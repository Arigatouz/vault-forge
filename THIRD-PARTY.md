# Third-party assets

Assets in this repository that are not authored here. Everything else is MIT (see `LICENSE`).
The generated vault includes two things that are not authored here:

- `.obsidian/themes/SynthWave/` is the SynthWave theme by marcoluzi, redistributed unchanged as the
  vault's default look. Check its own licence before relying on it in a commercial setting, and replace
  it with your own theme if the terms do not suit you.
- `.obsidian/snippets/` are CSS snippets written for this tool. Edit or delete them; the vault works
  without a theme.
